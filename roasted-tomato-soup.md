# Roasty Toma Soup with Halloumi Croutons

Via https://www.feastingathome.com/roasted-tomato-soup-haloumi-croutons-yogurt-sumac/

## Ingredients

* 3 pounds ripe tomatoes, halved, stem removed (preferably heirloom)
* ½ large red onion, sliced into thin discs
* 1 red bell pepper, cut into quarters
* 1 large carrot, thinly sliced
* 6 garlic cloves whole in skin with ends cut off
* 2 tsp fresh thyme leaves
* 1 tsp dried oregano
* ⅓ cup olive oil
* 3 cups no-chicken stock
* ¼ cup fresh basil leaves
* Salt and pepper to taste
* Pinch cayenne
* ½-2 tbsp honey
* ½ cup sour cream
* 1  6-8 ounce block of halloumi cheese, cut into ¾ inch cubes

## Procedure

1. Preheat oven to 425 F
2. On two parchement-lined sheet pans, spread out tomatoes (cut side down), onions,  bell pepper, carrot and garlic. Sprinkle with the herbs, drizzle with oil and give a good toss. Roast for 30-40 minutes, checking towards the end to make sure garlic doesn't burn (removing if need be). When done, remove from the oven and let cool for 5-10 minutes.
3. While the tomatoes are roasting, cut the  halloumi into small 3/4 inch cubes. Pat dry. Heat a tablespoon of oil in a skillet over medium-high heat and sear the halloumi on two sides, until golden, not overcrowding and turning heat down if necessary. Place on a paper towel-lined plate.
4. When the roasties are cooled, combine them (and their juices) with the broth and basil in a stock pot. Use an immersion blender to process until smooth.
5. Place in a pot, over low heat, add salt,  pepper, cayenne, a generous drizzle of olive oil and honey and bring to a gentle simmer.  Simmer for 5-10 minutes until soup reduces a bit and flavors meld.
6. Swirl in ½ cup sour cream
7. Serve soup in bowls garnished with a few halloumi croutons and basil chiffonade. It's good.
